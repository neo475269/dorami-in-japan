Naoshima: Yellow Pumpkin

直島

The ferry to Naoshima smelled like salt and diesel, and the whole island felt like it was holding its breath for art. Then there it was on the shore — Kusama's giant yellow pumpkin, all dots and sunshine, sitting on the sand like it had washed up and decided to stay.

I leaned against it for a photo and immediately regretted the pose, because a little boy ran up and asked, very seriously, whether the pumpkin was real and whether it grew here. His mum laughed; I told him it grew in an artist's imagination, which is basically the same thing in Naoshima.

I stayed until the light went soft over the Seto Inland Sea, and the pumpkin turned the colour of late-afternoon honey. Some islands you visit; this one feels like it lets you in on a secret.
