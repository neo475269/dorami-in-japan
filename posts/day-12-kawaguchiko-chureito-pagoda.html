Kawaguchiko: Chureito Pagoda

新倉山浅間公園 — 富士山を望む絶景と石段の旅

The stone steps smell like rain and fallen maple leaves — all 398 of them. I counted in bursts, forgot somewhere around 200, and then an old man in hiking gaiters crab-walked straight past me up the slope like it was nothing. So I stopped counting and just kept climbing.

Near the top, an obaasan selling sweet-potato manjū waved me over and pressed a free one into my hands — "because you look tired, girl." I was very tired. It was still warm in the middle, and somehow that fixed my legs.

And then — Fuji, huge and unhurried behind the red pagoda, exactly like every postcard you've ever seen but with your own lungs burning a little. I stood there grinning like an idiot until the wind took my receipt for breakfast. Worth every single step.
