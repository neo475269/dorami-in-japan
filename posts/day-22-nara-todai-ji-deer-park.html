Nara: Todai-ji & deer park

奈良・東大寺 — 鹿せんべいと大仏殿

The deer in Nara Park have no manners and no fear, and I love them for it. One bowed at me the second I held out a cracker — an actual polite little bow — then ate it and immediately headbutted my coat pocket looking for more, while maple leaves crunched under my boots and the great hall of Todai-ji loomed behind us like something out of a storybook.

The cracker lady laughed when a deer nibbled my scarf and told me, in very patient Japanese, that the deer are better behaved than most tourists. I chose not to take it personally, mostly because a deer was chewing my sleeve at the time, and she just handed me another pack with the air of someone who has seen this exact scene a thousand times.

Inside Todai-ji, the Great Buddha is so enormous it makes the whole hall feel like a different scale of world, and standing under it I felt tiny in the best possible way — like this trip keeps finding new ways to make me feel small and lucky at the same time.
