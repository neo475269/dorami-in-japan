19

Kyoto: Kiyomizu-dera

清水寺 — 木造の舞台から望む京都

I climbed up to Kiyomizu-dera just as the afternoon light turned everything gold, and honestly — the wooden stage is even more ridiculous in person than in photos. The whole hillside was on fire with maples, and below me Kyoto just sprawled out, all grey rooftops and one very photogenic pagoda poking up through the red.

A little girl next to me was counting the pagoda's roofs out loud in Japanese — ichi, ni, san — and her grandma kept correcting her with the patience of a saint. I found myself counting along under my breath too, and when we both got to five at the same time we burst out laughing. Neither of us spoke the other's language, but apparently counting to five is universal.

By the time I came back down Sannenzaka, the stone street was glowing in the late sun, and all the little shops smelled of yatsuhashi and roasted tea. I walked slowly, the way you do when you don't want a day to end yet. Kyoto keeps giving me these afternoons — I think I'm going to miss them when they're gone.
