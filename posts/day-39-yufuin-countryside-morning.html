39

Yufuin: countryside morning

由布院

I set my alarm for 5am in Yufuin, which is a sentence I never thought I would say on holiday, and honestly it was worth every stolen minute of sleep. The whole valley was still asleep under a blanket of mist — the rice fields silver and still, the air cold enough to see my breath — and then Mt. Yufu's twin peaks just rose out of it like the valley was showing off.

An old farmer in rubber boots was already out by the field, and he waved me over like we were old friends. He pointed at the mountain, said something about the fog lifting by eight, and then pressed a warm mandarin into my hand. I tried to say thank you in my best Japanese and he laughed so kindly I decided to take it as a compliment.

By the time I walked back toward the little main street, the mist was thinning and the rooftops were turning gold. Yufuin moves at the pace of steam rising off a rice field — slow, unbothered, and completely sure of itself. I think this might be my favourite morning of the whole trip so far.
