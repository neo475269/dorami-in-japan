51

Aomori: Hakkoda / bay

青森

Watch today's video on Google Drive

The snow started before I even got off the train — not proper snowfall, just the lightest dusting, like somebody shaking powdered sugar over the whole of Aomori. From the viewpoint above the bay, the Hakkoda peaks wear their first white caps of the season, and the bay below is grey-blue and calm, dotted with little fishing boats that look completely unbothered by the cold.

I bought a can of hot coffee from a vending machine at the top of the hill — the kind that burns through your gloves just enough to feel alive — and stood at the railing watching the flakes land on my beanie and melt. An old man walking his dog stopped beside me, pointed at the peaks, and said something about the snow coming early this year. I only caught half of it, but I nodded like a local and he laughed.

There is something about the first snow of a trip that feels like a reset — everything quieter, softer, a little more forgiving. My cheeks are pink, my nose is running, and I am completely, stupidly happy. Winter has officially started, and I am ready for it.
