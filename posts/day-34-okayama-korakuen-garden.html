Okayama: Korakuen Garden

岡山後楽園

Korakuen is the kind of garden that makes you slow down without asking permission. I walked in under a canopy of red maples, the leaves crunching softly under my boots, and there it was — the wide green lawn sloping down to the pond, with Okayama Castle standing black and elegant on the other side like it had been posing for centuries.

Then I spotted the crane. Just standing there on the grass, completely unbothered, as if it owned the place — which, honestly, it probably does. A gardener told me the cranes are raised here in the garden, and this one had wandered over to inspect the visitors. I stood very still and pretended I belonged too.

By late afternoon the light went honey-gold across the pond, and the castle's reflection wobbled gently on the water. I sat on the grass for a while, doing absolutely nothing, and it felt like the most productive thing I'd done all week.
