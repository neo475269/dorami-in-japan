Dorami in Japan

Day 1

Tokyo: Asakusa

Landed at Haneda at 7am with two suitcases and zero plans beyond this temple. Came straight to Asakusa because if you're going to start a three-month trip, you start where Tokyo is oldest.

The Kaminarimon lantern is even bigger in person — I stood under it grinning like a total tourist (because I am one) while an old man selling ningyo-yaki pointed at my Contax, gave me a thumbs up, and threw in an extra cake after a five-minute conversation conducted entirely in gestures.

Ninety days. The whole of Japan. My legs already ache and I haven't even left Asakusa. Let's go.
