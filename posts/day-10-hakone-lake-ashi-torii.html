Hakone: Lake Ashi torii

箱根・芦ノ湖 — 朝霧の湖面と富士山

I woke up at 5:40am because the ryokan owner swore Mt. Fuji only shows up for early risers — and she was right. The lake was so still it looked poured, and the torii floated in the mist like it hadn’t decided whether to be real yet.

An elderly photographer — the only other human on the dock — whispered ‘おはよう’ and waved me to the good spot, then spent ten minutes rearranging my coffee can on the planks like it was the main subject. We never exchanged names.

Sitting there while Fuji slowly lost its pink dawn glow, I felt properly small in the good way. Ten days in, and Japan keeps handing me mornings like this for free.
