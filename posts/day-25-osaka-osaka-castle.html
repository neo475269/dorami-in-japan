Osaka: Osaka Castle

大阪城 — 天守閣

Osaka Castle stopped me in my tracks before I even crossed the moat — the white walls and green-gold roofs rising over the huge stone walls, with maple trees flaming red and gold all around it. The air smelled like dry leaves and cold water, and every step along the moat path crunched under my boots.

An old man walking his tiny dog stopped beside me, pointed up at the golden shachihoko on the roof, and told me — in very patient, very slow Japanese — that the castle has burned down and been rebuilt more times than he can count. I understood maybe half of it and nodded like I understood all of it.

Standing there with the castle reflected in the moat and the whole park glowing around me, I felt that quiet, happy ache you only get on a really good travel day — like I could walk this park forever and still not be done looking.
