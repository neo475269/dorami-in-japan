Nikko: Kegon Falls

華厳の滝 — 霧の中の展望台

I could hear Kegon Falls before I could see it — this deep, endless roar through the cedar trees, and by the time I reached the viewing deck the mist had already settled on my beanie like tiny cold freckles.

An older obaasan next to me pointed at the falling water and said, '日本で一番綺麗な滝だよ' — the prettiest waterfall in Japan — then fished a dried persimmon out of her backpack and handed it to me like we'd been friends for years.

Standing there watching all that water just keep falling, the whole hillside burning red and gold around it, I got that small good kind of dizzy — the kind where your worries sound quieter than the water.
