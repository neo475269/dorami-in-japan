Takamatsu: Ritsurin Garden

栗林公園

Ritsurin Garden is the kind of place that makes you walk slower without deciding to. I crossed the arched bridge over the pond and stopped halfway, because the koi were gliding underneath in slow orange streaks and the sculpted pines looked like they'd been combed into place by someone very patient, a hundred years ago.

An old gardener was raking maple leaves off the moss, one careful sweep at a time, and he looked up and grinned when he saw me trying to catch a leaf mid-fall. "Slow," he said, holding up one finger — and he was right. This garden only works if you let it set the pace.

I sat on a bench by the pond until the light went honey-coloured and the whole garden went quiet around me. Some days on this trip feel like running; today felt like breathing out.
