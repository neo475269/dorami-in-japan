Tokyo: Shibuya

渋谷 — スクランブル交差点の夕暮れ

Came to Shibuya at dusk because the internet said the scramble crossing is best at blue hour, and for once the internet was right. Ten thousand people moving in every direction at once and somehow nobody bumps into anyone — it's the most polite chaos I've ever seen.

I stood on the edge with my camera out, looking extremely touristy, when a salaryman stopped mid-stride to point out the best corner for photos. "109 behind you! Very good!" he said, then dashed off into the crowd like a NPC with one line of dialogue.

Something about the neon reflecting on wet pavement makes this city feel electric and lonely at the same time. Day 2, and I'm already a little bit in love with Tokyo's noise.
